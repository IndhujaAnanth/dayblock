DAYBLOCK: put it online as an installable app (free)

Files in this folder (upload ALL of them, keep the same names):
  index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest,
  icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png

OPTION A - GitHub Pages (no commands needed)
 1. Sign in at github.com and click New repository. Name it dayblock, set it to Public, tick "Add a README", click Create.
 2. In the repository click Add file > Upload files. Drag in all the files above (not the folder itself). Click Commit changes.
 3. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment" choose Source: Deploy from a branch, Branch: main, folder /(root). Save.
 4. Wait about a minute. Your address will be:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/dayblock/

OPTION B - Netlify (even quicker)
 1. Go to app.netlify.com, sign up free, open Sites, and drag this whole folder onto the "Drag and drop your site output folder here" area.
 2. Netlify gives you an address like https://random-name.netlify.app. You can rename it in Site configuration.

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
 Android (Chrome): open the address, tap the three dots, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
 iPhone (Safari): open the address, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
 After the first visit it opens instantly from the icon, even with no internet.

MOVE YOUR OLD DATA ACROSS (the new address is a different place, so it starts empty)
 1. In the OLD Dayblock tap "Backup or restore" and "Copy backup".
 2. Open the NEW app, tap "Backup or restore", paste into "Restore from a backup", tap Restore.

IMPORTANT
 Your data is saved on that phone only. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it.
 Copy a backup now and then (the app reminds you) and send it to yourself.

UPDATING LATER
 When you change index.html, also change VERSION in sw.js (dayblock-v1 to dayblock-v2), then upload both files again.
